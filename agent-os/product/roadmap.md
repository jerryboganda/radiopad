# Product Roadmap

## Phase 1: MVP and current platform hardening

- Tenant-aware authentication and role-based access.
- Structured report editor with findings, impression, recommendations, autosave, and
  explicit sign-off.
- AI drafting and rewrite flows through the single gateway, with visible review state and
  auditable provenance.
- Versioned YAML rulebooks, validation findings, golden-case coverage, and rulebook
  authoring.
- Template library, provider registry, usage metering, governance views, and append-only
  audit chain.
- Text, JSON, PDF, DOCX, and FHIR DiagnosticReport export.
- Windows desktop packaging and updater; Android/iOS companion dictation and pairing.
- .NET CLI support for daemon, rulebook validation/testing, report generation, and audit
  export.

## Phase 2: Post-launch expansion

- Production PACS/DICOMweb and FHIR/HL7 integrations.
- Enterprise SSO, SCIM, customer-managed keys, and stronger deployment controls.
- Expanded offline/local-model workflows and on-device dictation.
- Mature billing, operational analytics, and multi-site governance.
- Additional approved clinical rulebooks and licensed terminology integrations.
