# Product Mission

## Problem

Radiologists need a fast, trustworthy reporting workspace that reduces repetitive drafting
without weakening clinical review, auditability, tenant isolation, or control of the final
signed report. Existing workflows split reporting, dictation, validation, and AI assistance
across disconnected tools.

## Target Users

- Radiologists who draft, validate, review, sign, and export reports.
- Clinical teams that share templates, rulebooks, and dictated findings.
- Platform administrators who manage tenants, providers, governance, billing, and usage.
- Developers and operators who need a local-first desktop, mobile companion, web admin
  surface, and offline-capable CLI.

## Solution

RadioPad is an AI-assisted radiology reporting platform delivered as a Next.js web surface,
Windows Tauri desktop app, Android/iOS mobile companion, ASP.NET Core API, and .NET CLI.
It combines structured reports, rulebook validation, reviewed AI drafts, dictation,
interoperability exports, and append-only audit evidence while keeping the radiologist in
control of the final report.
