## What RadioPad does

RadioPad turns dictation, structured findings, prior reports, measurements and study metadata into a draft radiology report. It checks the draft against your institution's rulebooks and exports the signed report as a FHIR R4 DiagnosticReport, PDF, DOCX or plain text.

Reporting happens in the Windows desktop app. The mobile companion carries voice dictation into a report open on a paired desktop. The web admin console is for administrators and does not include reporting.

## Clinical responsibility

RadioPad is a drafting and validation tool. It does not read images, make diagnoses or sign reports.

- Every report must be reviewed and signed by a qualified radiologist, who is responsible for its content.
- AI-drafted text is marked until it has been reviewed or edited. Treat it as a draft until you have read it.
- Rulebook validation checks the rules your institution has written. A draft that passes validation is not thereby correct, and validation does not assess the images.
