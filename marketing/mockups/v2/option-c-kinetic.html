How it works

Four steps from voice to a signed report.

  2. 01

    Dictate

    Speak at the desktop or into your paired phone. The local engine transcribes while you read the study.

  3. 02

    Draft

    Dictation, priors, measurements and study metadata become a structured draft in your template.

  4. 03

    Validate

    Your rulebook checks laterality, required sections and critical results before anything is final.

  5. 04

    Sign

    You review, edit and sign. Then export FHIR R4, PDF, DOCX or plain text.
