Workflow

Four steps from dictation to a signed report.

RadioPad does the drafting and the checking. Reading the study and signing the report stay with the radiologist.

01

Dictate

Speak at the workstation with a hotkey, or pair your phone to the live desktop session and dictate from the reading room chair.

DictatingMobile companion, paired to desktop

"Six millimeter solid nodule, right lower lobe, image one forty two. No consolidation. No effusion.

Hotkey hold to dictate on desktop, or tap the mic on your phone.

02

Draft

Dictation, structured findings, prior reports, measurements and study metadata go in. A complete draft comes out, in your template.

03

Validate

Every draft runs against your institution's rulebook. Blockers stop sign-off until they are resolved. Warnings and info stay visible.

Sign

Validation engine

Your department's rules, checked before every signature.

Rulebooks are written per institution and versioned like code. RadioPad flags the draft, the radiologist decides.

FINDINGS6 mm solid nodule in the right lower lobe.IMPRESSION6 mm solid nodule, left lower lobe.
BlockerSide terms disagree between findings and impression.

severity: blocker

Security and governance

Every AI call leaves a record. No report leaves unsigned.

The AI never signs

Signing is a radiologist action. There is no setting that changes that.
