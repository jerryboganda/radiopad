02Validation

Your rulebook reads every draft before you do.

Rulebooks are plain YAML that your thoracic, neuro or body committee owns. RadioPad checks every draft against them, and a blocker keeps the sign button off until it is fixed.

# rulebooks/chest_ct_v1.yaml
- id: laterality_consistency
  severity: blocker
  description: Side-specific findings must
    agree between the Findings and the
    Impression.
Blocker

Impression says left, findings say right.Signing stays disabled until the side is corrected.

Findings6 mm solid nodule, right lower lobe.
ImpressionStable 6 mm left lower lobe nodule.
# rulebooks/chest_ct_v1.yaml
required_sections:
  - Indication
  - Technique
  - Comparison
  - Findings
  - Impression
Blocker

Comparison section is missing.Add the prior study or state that none is available.

PresentIndication, Technique, Findings, Impression
MissingComparison
# rulebooks/chest_ct_v1.yaml
- id: critical_result_language
  severity: blocker
  description: Time-critical findings need
    documented communication with read-back,
    naming the recipient and the time.
Critical result

Large right pneumothorax with no communication recorded.Name who was told, confirm read-back, and add the time.

FindingsLarge right pneumothorax with mediastinal shift.
NeedsRecipient · read-back · time
# rulebooks/chest_ct_v1.yaml
- id: measurement_consistency
  severity: warning
  description: Every nodule dimension must
    be reported identically wherever it
    appears.
Warning

Findings say 6 mm, impression says 8 mm.Pick one size and use it everywhere, including the comparison sentence.

Findings6 mm solid nodule, right lower lobe.
ImpressionStable 8 mm right lower lobe nodule.

$ radiopad rulebook validate chest_ct_v1.yaml

✓ required_sections: Indication, Technique, Comparison, Findings, Impression

06Governance

Governed like clinical software should be.

Every AI call leaves a record. Every signature belongs to a person. Your data stays where you deploy it.
